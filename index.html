Vibe neuroimaging

These are real brains and real scans with zero homework. Eleven little games were built from open brain data. Each one is a single file and needs no internet. They were made in the D'Mello lab with the lab's own analysis tools. Everything was computed on the actual 3-D scans. The pretty brain pictures are only pictures.

Adopt a brainTen people were scanned on ten midnights. You get one mystery night. Whose brain is it?
14 MBBrain travelWe drop a pin on one spot of a ten-person average brain and show you its ten best friends. Two networks are on the table, each with its usual friends map under its name. Spot the match. Beat the coin.
10 MBWatch a movie through a brainFourteen people watched Forrest Gump inside a very loud tube. You get two scenes as two brain maps. Which one had the 14 brains more in step? The brighter map is the answer by definition, and a coin is your only opponent.
22 MBGrow upKids from three to twelve and grown-ups watched a Pixar short. You get one brain. How old is this person?
6 MBMind readingFive people stared at 50 objects dozens of times each. You get three brain blips of one picture, averaged into a map, and two names with their usual maps. Spot the match by eye. Then watch the mind reader do it nine times out of ten.
5 MBForty brains hear one storyForty strangers heard the same seven-minute story. You get two moments of it as two brain maps, each with its sentence. Which one gripped the brains more? The brighter map wins by definition, and you play against a coin.
19 MBCan a brain tell a famous face from a stranger?Eight people looked at famous faces, strangers and scrambled mush. You get three runs of one kind averaged into a map, two kinds to pick from and each kind's usual map under its name. Spot the match. Faces are easy and fame is nearly a coin flip.
8 MBWatch the brain's reply to a faceA face flashes for less than a second and the brain's reply swells and fades over fourteen seconds. You get three people's frames of it, averaged, and two moments with their usual frames. Is this the climb or the fade?
9 MBGuess the person141 young adults did nothing in a scanner. You get three of their resting brains averaged into one map and a two-answer question: all women or all men, the youngest or the oldest, all worriers or none. Two of the three can be done.
5 MBDoctor modeThree patients who share one diagnosis are averaged into one brain map. Two diagnoses are on the table, each with its group's usual brain map. Spot the match. Beat the computer. Beat Dr. Nothing-Wrong.
5 MBWhat is this blob for?Open a pack of five cards. Two blobs face each other on a brain painted by team, and you say which is rarer. Then one blob faces two game pages. Beat the collector, who only ever goes big. Only 18 of 434 blobs are legendary.
5 MBAtlas explorerThe plain map. Hover around and learn what things are called.
